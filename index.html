13 / FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

Contact us →
What agricultural consultancy services do you provide?

We provide practical agricultural guidance, field inspection, pest and disease diagnosis, product recommendation, technical guidance and farmer support.

Do you provide field inspection?

Yes. Field inspection is part of our agricultural support approach, focused on observing field and crop conditions.

Can you help identify crop pests and diseases?

Yes. We provide pest and disease diagnosis and assessment as part of our agricultural consultancy services.

Do you supply agricultural inputs?

Yes. We provide agricultural input supply and recommendation based on crop and field requirements.

Do you provide seedlings?

Yes. We produce and supply agricultural seedlings. Availability varies by season.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Ichchakamana-03, Chitwan and serve local and regional markets across central Nepal, including Chitwan, Dhading, Pokhara and Kathmandu.

Do you sell fresh eggs wholesale?

Yes. Fresh eggs are available for retail and wholesale customers.

Can consumers purchase eggs directly?

Yes. Fresh eggs are supplied to consumers as well as wholesale buyers.

Do you deliver agricultural products?

Agricultural product delivery is part of our market supply activities. Contact us to discuss your requirement.
