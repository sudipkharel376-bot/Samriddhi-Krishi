About Us

An agribusiness built for farmers, food and markets

Samriddhi Krishi and Poultry Pvt. Ltd. is a private, profit-oriented company registered on 2079/04/19 B.S. and based in Ichchakamana–03, Chitwan, Nepal. The company brings together agricultural consultancy, seedling production, crop production, and layer poultry production under one integrated operation.

Business philosophy

Technical knowledge, then food, then market

Samriddhi is not a single-purpose operation. It is not merely an agrovet, a poultry farm, a vegetable farm, or a consultancy on its own — it is an integrated, market-oriented agribusiness that connects each of these together:

Technical knowledge → Farmer support → Seedlings → Crop production → Poultry production → Fresh food → Market → Consumer

The company works directly with farmers on one side (Business to Farmer), while producing and supplying fresh vegetables and eggs to consumers, retailers, and wholesale buyers on the other (Business to Consumer / Business).

Agricultural Consultancy

Practical guidance from people who work in the field

A crop problem rarely waits. Our agricultural team works with farmers directly — on-site where needed — to understand what's happening in the field and recommend what actually helps.

Request Agricultural Consultation
Why it matters

Small problems, caught early, cost less

A misdiagnosed pest, the wrong product, or a missed disease symptom can affect an entire season's yield. Agricultural consultation connects farmers with practical, field-tested guidance before small issues become large losses.

Field Inspection

Our team observes crop and field conditions directly, rather than diagnosing from a description alone.

Pest & Disease Diagnosis

We identify and assess the pest or disease problem affecting the crop.

Product Recommendation

Based on the crop's requirements and the problem observed, we recommend suitable agricultural products.

Technical Guidance

Ongoing, practical advice on production and farm management decisions.

How it works

Our consultation process

A general workflow — steps may be adjusted case by case depending on the crop and problem.

1

Contact Us

Reach out with a short description of your crop and the problem you're seeing.

2

Understand the Problem

Our team gathers initial details — crop type, symptoms, field conditions and timeline.

3

Field Inspection

Where needed, our team visits the field to observe conditions directly.

4

Diagnosis & Assessment

We identify and assess the pest, disease, or agronomic issue affecting the crop.

5

Recommendation

We recommend suitable agricultural products and practices based on the diagnosis.

6

Follow-up Guidance

Continued support as the crop responds to treatment and moves through the season.
